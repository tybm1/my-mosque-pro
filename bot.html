MyMosqueProBot

MyMosqueProBot reads the jamaat (congregation) times that UK mosques publish on their own websites, so worshippers can see accurate daily times in the My Mosque Pro app. Every time shown in the app links back to the mosque's own website.

How it behaves

  • It visits a mosque's prayer-times page about once a day, usually around 4am UK time.
  • It makes at most one request every few seconds to any website, and doesn't download images, video or fonts.
  • It obeys robots.txt. If a site disallows it, or refuses access, it stops visiting that site.
  • It identifies itself as MyMosqueProBot with a link to this page.

Stop or correct it

To stop the bot visiting your site, add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: MyMosqueProBot
Disallow: /

If your mosque's times look wrong in the app, or you'd like us to stop listing them, email info@mymosquepro.com and we'll act on it promptly.
