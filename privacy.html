My Mosque Pro

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026 · Soul Sync Solutions CIC

My Mosque Pro helps you find mosques in the UK and see their prayer and jamaat times. We collect as little as possible.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your location on your device to show mosques near you and the Qibla direction. Your location isn't sent to us or stored by us.

Saved on your device

The mosques you follow, your prayer streak and your settings are stored on your phone. They aren't sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Updates you send

If you report a jamaat time change, we store the mosque, prayer, time and date you send, so our team can check it. It isn't linked to you.

If you ask to manage a mosque's listing ("I run this mosque"), we store the name, role and phone number or email you give, only to check the request and contact you about it. We delete these details within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Donations

Donations are handled by the payment provider shown at checkout (for example Stripe or the mosque's own donation page). We don't receive or store your card details.

Where data is kept

Updates you send are stored securely with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in London. We don't sell data or use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything you've sent us by emailing info@mymosquepro.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
